Threats to validity — BERTScore truncation: We computed BERTScore using microsoft/deberta-xlarge-mnli with a tokenizer limit of 512 tokens. Texts longer than this limit were truncated before scoring. Across N=5526 AI–human pairs, truncation affected 8.54% of pairs (AI side: 3.60%, human side: 5.21%). In total, truncation removed 57,966 tokens from pair inputs (2.61% token loss; AI=29,274/3.39%, Human=28,692/2.12%). The highest pair-level truncation rate was 26.82% for model=gpt-5-mini-2025-08-07 under context=File Contents, while the largest token removal volume was 12,053 tokens for model=gpt-5-mini-2025-08-07 under context=File Contents. Therefore, BERTScore values for long descriptions may under-represent content appearing beyond the first 512 tokens.
